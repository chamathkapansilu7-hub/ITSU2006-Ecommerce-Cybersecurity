Project Scope and Work Breakdown Structure

This document defines the scope and WBS for the proposed E-Commerce Cybersecurity Risk Assessment and Mitigation Strategy project.

In Scope: Security asset identification, threat analysis, risk assessment, mitigation planning, governance and reporting.

Out of Scope: Production deployment, live penetration testing and development of a complete e-commerce application.

Major Work Packages: Initiation, research, project planning, risk analysis, security control planning, governance and project closure.
